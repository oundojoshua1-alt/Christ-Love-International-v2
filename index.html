Who We Are

About Christ Love International

Christ at the center. Love in action.

Christ Love International is a Christ-centered mission committed to sharing the Gospel of Jesus Christ and demonstrating His love through practical service.

We believe transformation begins with Christ and extends into every area of life. Through evangelism, discipleship, mentorship, empowerment and community outreach, we desire to see people discover hope, purpose and a future grounded in God.

Our heart is especially focused on young people and vulnerable communities, while building partnerships that allow the mission to reach further across Kenya, Africa and beyond.

“Let all that you do be done in love.”

1 Corinthians 16:14

Our Heart

We don't want the Gospel to remain only a message we speak. We want it to be a life we live — expressed through compassion, service, discipleship, integrity and love.
