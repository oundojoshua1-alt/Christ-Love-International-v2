Who We Are

About Christ Love International

We are a Christ-centered non-profit organization committed to impacting lives, especially the young generation, through the message of the Gospel and practical acts of Love and service. Founded on Biblical truth, we seek to fulfill the Great Commission by nurturing faith, offering hope, and restoring dignity to individuals and communities.

1 Corinthians 13:13

"And now these three remain: faith, hope and love. But the greatest of these is love."

John 10:10

"I came that they may have life and have it abundantly."

Our Vision

To see a transformed generation living purposefully in Christ, spreading His love, and positively impacting communities across Kenya and beyond.

Our Mission

To share the Gospel of Jesus Christ, mentor youth, empower communities, and demonstrate God’s love through holistic ministry—touching spiritual, emotional, and physical needs.

Foundational Pillars

Our 6 Core Values

1. Christ-Centeredness

Jesus Christ is the center and foundation of everything we do.

2. Unconditional Love

Serving all people with kindness, empathy, and respect without discrimination.

3. Integrity & Transparency

Upholding honesty, accountability, and ethical standards in all operations.

4. Servant Leadership

Leading by serving others, following the example of Jesus Christ.

5. Community Empowerment

Building capacity and creating sustainable change for future generations.

6. Excellence

Striving for the highest quality in ministry, service, and organizational work.
