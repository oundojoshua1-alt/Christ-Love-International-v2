Who We Are

About Christ Love International

A Christ-centered mission committed to sharing the Gospel, making disciples, mentoring lives, empowering communities and demonstrating the love of Christ through practical service.

Love that leads to Christ.

Christ Love International exists to make the love of Jesus visible in both word and action.

We believe the Gospel changes lives, and that genuine love should move us to care for people spiritually, emotionally, socially and practically.

Our desire is simple: to point people to Christ, walk with them in faith and help communities flourish by God's grace and for His glory.

Our Heart

We are passionate about young people, vulnerable communities, discipleship, evangelism, mentorship, education, skills development and practical compassion.

From Kenya to the nations, we desire to serve people, create opportunities and proclaim the hope found in Jesus Christ.

Where We Are Going

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To see lives and communities transformed by the love, truth and hope of Jesus Christ, with empowered young people becoming faithful leaders who impact their communities and beyond.

Our Mission

To proclaim the Gospel, make disciples, mentor young people, serve vulnerable communities, develop skills and create opportunities while demonstrating the love of Christ through practical action.
